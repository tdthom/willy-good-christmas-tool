Roof Measure (Top View)

Use a satellite or drone screenshot to measure ridge, hip, and eaves from above. These measurements are what feed the Estimate tab.

  1. Upload a top-down screenshot of the roof.
  2. Click Set scale and draw a line of known length (e.g., 40 ft).
  3. Select run type and click along roof lines in Measure runs mode.
